# Lessons

## Active rules

- Optimize for named, source-backed entities, not thematic relevance.
- Exact recency windows should be enforced with `after:YYYY-MM-DD` in the query.
- Resolve official domains and LinkedIn company URLs only after a company is a real candidate.
- Publisher hits are useful as evidence, but they are not enough on their own if the company identity stays ambiguous.
